# Chicken Fettuccine Alfredo

Based on [To Die For Fettuccine Alfredo (Allrecipes)](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/23431/to-die-for-fettuccine-alfredo/). The original recipe (1X) yields 6 servings. 2X fed 11 people. The noodles and sauce are the main filler; the chicken is an add-on.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | 1X (6 servings) | 2X (11 people) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dry fettuccine pasta (linguine works too) | 1½ lb (24 oz) | 3 lb |
| Butter | 1 cup | 2 cups |
| Heavy cream | ¾ pint | 1½ pints |
| Garlic salt | 1 dash | 2 pinches |
| Salt and ground black pepper | to taste | to taste |
| Grated Romano cheese | ¾ cup | 1½ cups |
| Grated Parmesan cheese | ½ cup | 1 cup |

## Chicken (my addition)

- Chicken thighs: about 3 lb for 11 people.
- Marinate in McCormick Grill Mates Garden Herb marinade (1 packet).

## Method

From the Allrecipes page:

1. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Add the fettuccine and cook 8 to 10 minutes, until al dente; drain.
2. Melt the butter into the cream in a large saucepan over low heat; add salt, pepper, and garlic salt.
3. Increase the heat to medium; stir in the grated Romano and Parmesan until melted and the sauce has thickened.
4. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and toss until thoroughly coated; serve immediately.
