A dish worth making again. Put the source link in `url`, the original yield in `servings`, and the most recent cook date in `last_made`. Tag with `recipe` plus the course or main ingredient (e.g. `pasta`, `dinner`). Each recipe gets ingredients, method, a shopping list, and a dated history.
