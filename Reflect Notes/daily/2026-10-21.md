## Journal Entries

### Journal Entry — Gmail Integration Research

Spent some time today digging into the Gmail integration for the email system. Good news overall — I think we have a real path forward.

The original reason we went with SES was Gmail's attachment limits, and that's still a thing: the Gmail API caps sending at 35MB total (~25MB of actual attachments). Google raised limits to 50MB earlier this year, but only for Enterprise Plus and only through the web UI, so it doesn't help us. SES still wins at 40MB anyway.

But here's the interesting part — we don't have to pick one or the other. The plan is a hybrid: keep SES as the delivery engine, and use the Gmail API's `messages.insert` endpoint to mirror a copy into the user's Sent folder. Insert just writes the message directly into their mailbox without Gmail transmitting anything, and it allows up to 150MB, so even our biggest SES messages mirror fine. Best of both worlds — SES does the heavy lifting, Gmail stays the user's source of truth.

For inbound, replies land in the user's Gmail naturally since the From address is theirs. We can use `users.watch` with Google Pub/Sub push notifications to sync replies back into the app — no polling.

Big win: since we're on Google Workspace and this is internal-only, we can set the OAuth consent screen to Internal and skip Google's whole app verification/security review process. That was looking like the most painful part. Also solves the authentication problem — we'll add SES to our domain's SPF record and set up DKIM, so SES-sent mail from our domain won't trip spam filters.
