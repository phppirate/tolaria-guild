# Checkride Maneuvers - ACS Tolerances

#flying #checkride
Assistant: [[Maverick]]

Private pilot checkride prep. C172N N73785, Kalispell (S27, ~2,930 ft). Instructor: [[Preston Nelson]]. Dual practice Thu Oct 22, 2026, 4-6 PM MT.

Source: [FAA-S-ACS-6C Private Pilot Airplane ACS](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf)

## Airport and takeoff/landing (Areas III-IV)

- **III.B Traffic pattern:** altitude ±100 ft, airspeed ±10 kt
- **IV.A Normal takeoff:** Vy +10/-5 kt
- **IV.B Normal landing:** approach speed (or ≤1.3 Vso) +10/-5 kt; touch down within 400 ft beyond the point, no drift
- **IV.C Soft-field takeoff:** Vx/Vy +10/-5 kt
- **IV.D Soft-field landing:** approach +10/-5 kt; no touchdown distance standard
- **IV.E Short-field takeoff:** Vx/obstacle-clearance speed +10/-5 kt until 50 ft AGL, then Vy +10/-5 kt
- **IV.F Short-field landing:** approach +10/-5 kt; touch down within **200 ft** beyond the point (common bust)
- **IV.M Forward slip to landing:** touch down within 400 ft beyond the point
- **IV.N Go-around:** Vx/Vy +10/-5 kt, then Vy +10/-5 kt

## Performance and ground reference (Area V)

- **V.A Steep turns:** ~45° bank, 360° each direction; altitude ±100 ft, airspeed ±10 kt, bank ±5°, rollout ±10°; enter at or below Va
- **V.B Ground reference:** 600-1,000 ft AGL; altitude ±100 ft, airspeed ±10 kt

## Navigation (Area VI)

- **VI.C Diversion:** altitude ±200 ft, heading ±15°

## Slow flight and stalls (Area VII)

- **VII.A Slow flight:** complete at or above 1,500 AGL; altitude ±100 ft, heading ±10°, airspeed +10/-0 kt, bank ±10°; no stall warning
- **VII.B/C Power-off / power-on stalls:** full stall, recover per POH; heading ±10° or bank ≤20° ±10° in turning stalls; complete at or above 1,500 AGL
- **VII.D Spin awareness:** knowledge only

## Basic instrument maneuvers (Area VIII)

- Altitude ±200 ft, heading ±20°, airspeed ±10 kt
- Turns: rollout ±10°
- Unusual attitudes: recover by reference to instruments

## Emergencies (Area IX)

- **IX.A Emergency descent:** bank 30-45°, airspeed +0/-10 kt, level-off ±100 ft
- **IX.B Emergency approach and landing:** best glide ±10 kt

## Practice notes (instructor practice, not ACS)

- Typical flow: steep turns → slow flight/stalls → hood work → simulated engine failure → ground reference → landings
- Work at ~4,500-5,500 MSL to stay at or above 1,500 AGL over terrain near S27
- POH V-speeds (Vx, Vy, Va, Vso, best glide) for N73785 are pulled from the Red Eagle Aviation Drive POH below.
- Mid-October density altitude near field elevation

### POH reference speeds (1977 C172N; unchanged by STC SA4428SW) [POH]

- Normal climb-out, flaps up: 70-80 KIAS [p. 4-3]
- Short-field takeoff, flaps up, speed at 50 ft: 59 KIAS [p. 4-3]. With 10° flaps: 55 KIAS obstacle-clearance speed [p. 4-15]
- Vy (best rate of climb): 73 KIAS at sea level / 68 KIAS at 10,000 ft [p. 4-3]
- Vx (best angle of climb): 59 KIAS at sea level / 61 KIAS at 10,000 ft [p. 4-3]
- En-route climb: 75-85 KIAS at sea level / 70-80 KIAS at 10,000 ft [p. 4-3]
- Normal approach: flaps up 60-70 KIAS; flaps 40° 55-65 KIAS [p. 4-3]
- Short-field approach, flaps 40°: 60 KIAS [p. 4-3]
- Balked landing: max power, flaps 20°, 55 KIAS [p. 4-3]
- Va (maneuvering speed): 97 KIAS at 2,300 lb / 89 KIAS at 1,950 lb / 80 KIAS at 1,600 lb [p. 2-4, Fig. 2-1; same figures as turbulent-air penetration speed, p. 4-3, and Section 3 airspeeds, p. 3-3]
- Best glide: 65 KIAS at 2,300 lb (flaps up) [p. 3-3]
- Max demonstrated crosswind: 15 kt [p. 4-3]
- Vfe (max flap extended): 85 KIAS [p. 2-4]; white arc 41-85 KIAS, green arc 47-128 KIAS [p. 2-5]
- Vno 128 KIAS, Vne 160 KIAS [p. 2-4]
- Stall speeds, power off, 2,300 lb, wings level [p. 5-11, Fig. 5-3]:
  - **Vs1 (flaps up): 47 KIAS** at most forward CG (42 KIAS at most rearward CG)
  - **Vso (flaps 40°): 41 KIAS** at most forward CG (36 KIAS at most rearward CG)
  - Flaps 10°: 44 KIAS forward CG / 38 KIAS rearward CG
  - At 45° bank (forward CG): 56 KIAS flaps up / 49 KIAS flaps 40°. At 60°: 66 / 58 KIAS
  - Altitude loss in a stall recovery may be as much as 180 ft. KIAS values are approximate.
- Rotate (normal takeoff): lift nose wheel at 55 KIAS [p. 4-7]

### Clearing turns (all airwork) [AFH]

- Before every maneuver, make two 90° turns (or one 180° turn) to clear the area. Look for traffic above, below, and around the airplane, and pick an emergency landing field.
- Pre-maneuver flow: fuel selector BOTH, mixture as required, carb heat as required, lights on, seat belts secure, altitude at or above 1,500 AGL (≈4,500+ MSL near S27).

### Steep turns (V.A)

- **Power:** ~2,100-2,200 RPM (**typical**; the POH gives no steep-turn setting, and the 180 hp engine may need less RPM than a stock 160 hp 172N; confirm with Preston). Add ~100-200 RPM as you roll in to hold airspeed.
- **Flaps:** up.
- **Airspeed:** at or below Va for your actual weight: 97/89/80 KIAS at 2,300/1,950/1,600 lb [POH p. 2-4]. Typical entry is ~90-95 KIAS (**typical**).
- **Trim:** trim for level flight at entry. Some CFIs add a little nose-up trim in the turn (**typical**).
- **Entry:** clearing turns → pick a heading/reference point → roll smoothly to 45° → as you pass ~30° of bank, add back pressure (and power) to hold altitude [AFH]. 45°-bank stall speed is 56 KIAS flaps up (forward CG) [POH p. 5-11].
- **Recovery:** start the rollout about half the bank angle (≈20°) before the entry heading. Release back pressure and reduce power to the entry setting. Reverse into the other direction if asked.

### Slow flight (VII.A)

- **Power:** reduce to ~1,500 RPM (**typical**). Carb heat ON before any significant power reduction [POH p. 4-19 note]. Once at speed, add power as needed to hold altitude (often ~1,800-2,000 RPM, **typical**).
- **Flaps:** extend in stages below 85 KIAS (Vfe) [POH p. 2-4] to the configuration the examiner asks for (often full).
- **Airspeed:** a speed at which any further increase in AOA, load factor, or power reduction would trigger the stall warning. The ACS requires no stall warning. In practice, use ~5-10 kt above the stall-warning onset (**typical**; no POH value). Reference: Vs1 47 / Vso 41 KIAS (forward CG) [POH p. 5-11].
- **Trim:** trim nose-up continually as speed decays.
- **Entry:** clearing turns → carb heat → power back → hold altitude with pitch while slowing → flaps in stages → set power for altitude. Pitch controls airspeed and power controls altitude [AFH].
- **Recovery:** full power, carb heat cold → lower the nose to hold altitude → retract flaps in stages (20° first, then remaining as you pass Vx/Vy) → cruise.

### Power-off stall (VII.B; approach/landing configuration)

- **Power:** carb heat ON, reduce to ~1,500 RPM (**typical**) while slowing to approach speed, then to idle.
- **Flaps:** landing configuration (often full; ask the examiner).
- **Airspeed:** stabilized approach at 55-65 KIAS flaps 40° [POH p. 4-3], then idle. Expect the stall near Vso 41 KIAS (forward CG) [POH p. 5-11].
- **Trim:** trim for approach speed.
- **Entry:** clearing turns → configure and descend at approach speed → idle → raise the nose smoothly to a landing attitude → hold it until the full stall (buffet / nose drop). Keep coordinated.
- **Recovery [AFH/POH]:** reduce AOA (forward elevator) → full power, carb heat cold → wings level with coordinated rudder → flaps to 20° → positive climb → retract the rest of the flaps in stages → climb at Vx/Vy (59/73 KIAS) [POH p. 4-3]. Altitude loss may be up to 180 ft [POH p. 5-11].

### Power-on stall (VII.C; takeoff/departure configuration)

- **Power:** slow to rotation/liftoff speed with reduced power (~1,500 RPM, **typical**), then full power (or as specified, at least 65% per the AFH).
- **Flaps:** up (takeoff configuration).
- **Airspeed:** slow to ~55-60 KIAS (**typical**; the POH rotate speed is 55 KIAS [p. 4-7]) before adding power. Expect the stall near Vs1 47 KIAS (forward CG) [POH p. 5-11].
- **Trim:** trim for the climb as you slow.
- **Entry:** clearing turns → power back, slow to near liftoff speed → set power → pitch up smoothly to the stall, using right rudder for coordination (expect more left-turning tendency with the 180 hp engine).
- **Recovery:** reduce AOA → full power (if not already) → level the wings → climb at Vx/Vy → return to cruise.

### Ground reference maneuvers (V.B: rectangular course, S-turns, turns around a point)

- **Power:** ~2,100-2,300 RPM, about 90-100 KIAS (**typical**; the POH has no ground-reference setting, and the 180 hp engine may need different RPM; confirm with Preston).
- **Flaps:** up. **Trim:** trim for level flight.
- **Altitude:** 600-1,000 ft AGL (≈3,600-3,900 MSL near S27). Pick a field with no obstacles and a forced-landing option.
- **Entry:** clearing turns → determine wind → enter downwind at 45° to the boundary or reference (or perpendicular for S-turns). The steepest bank goes where groundspeed is highest (downwind), and should not exceed ~45° [AFH].
- **Recovery/exit:** exit on the same heading as entry and climb back to practice altitude.

### Emergency descent (IX.A)

- **Power:** idle, carb heat ON [AFH: reduce power to idle; POH p. 4-19: carb heat before closing the throttle].
- **Flaps/speed:** the 1977 172N POH has no emergency-descent checklist (Section 3 checked), and the STC supplement leaves Section 3 unchanged [STC p. 6 of 8]. Use the AFH technique: clean up to Vno 128 KIAS (smooth air, up to Vne 160) [POH p. 2-4], or flaps extended at or below Vfe 85 KIAS. Which configuration the examiner expects is **instructor preference; ask Preston**.
- **Entry:** clearing turn → idle → roll into a 30-45° bank to keep positive load and see below → lower the nose for the target speed.
- **Recovery:** start the level-off ~100 ft above the target altitude → roll wings level → pitch to level → power to cruise → carb heat cold.

### Engine-out / best glide (IX.B, IX.C)

- **Airspeed:** **65 KIAS best glide** [POH p. 3-3]. Trim for it immediately.
- **Flaps:** up until landing is assured, then as needed (40° recommended for the forced landing; 60 KIAS with flaps down) [POH p. 3-4].
- **Flow [POH emergency checklist pp. 3-3/3-4 + AFH]:** pitch for 65 → pick a field (into the wind if possible) → restart checklist (carb heat ON, fuel selector BOTH, mixture RICH, ignition BOTH or START if the prop has stopped, primer IN and locked) → declare/squawk 7700 (simulate) → before landing: mixture idle cutoff, fuel OFF, ignition OFF, flaps as required, master OFF, doors unlatched, touchdown slightly tail low, brakes heavily.
- **Recovery (simulated):** at the CFI/DPE call, carb heat cold, full power, climb at Vy 73 KIAS [POH p. 4-3].

### Normal takeoff and landing (IV.A/B)

- **Takeoff [POH]:** flaps UP, carb heat cold, full throttle [p. 4-7]. Normal and short-field takeoffs use flaps up; 10° is for minimum ground run or soft/rough fields; more than 10° is not approved [p. 4-14]. Mixture rich; before takeoff from fields above 3,000 ft elevation, lean for max RPM in a full-throttle static runup [p. 4-14] (climb checklist: above 3,000 ft, lean to max RPM [p. 4-8]). **S27 is at 2,930 ft, and the density altitude will often be above 3,000 ft.** Lift the nose wheel at 55 KIAS [p. 4-7] → climb at 70-80 KIAS normal or 73 KIAS Vy [p. 4-3].
- **Landing [POH p. 4-9]:** approach 60-70 KIAS flaps up / 55-65 KIAS flaps 40°. Flaps as desired below 85 KIAS. Touch down main wheels first, lower the nosewheel gently, minimum braking.
- **Typical pattern power:** ~2,000-2,100 RPM downwind. Abeam the numbers: carb heat on, ~1,500 RPM, flaps 10° (**typical**; the POH gives no pattern RPM, and the 180 hp engine may need less; confirm with Preston).

### Short-field takeoff and landing (IV.E/F)

- **Takeoff [POH p. 4-8]:** flaps up, carb heat cold, brakes held, full throttle, mixture rich (above 3,000 ft, lean to max RPM), release brakes, elevator slightly tail-low, **climb at 59 KIAS until all obstacles are cleared**, then accelerate to Vy 73 KIAS. With 10° flaps, use a 55 KIAS obstacle-clearance speed [p. 4-15].
- **Landing [POH p. 4-9]:** 60-70 KIAS flaps up, then flaps 40°, **60 KIAS** until the flare, power to idle once the obstacle is cleared, touch down on the main wheels first, heavy braking, flaps up for max brake effectiveness [p. 4-20]. ACS: within 200 ft of the point.

### Soft-field takeoff and landing (IV.C/D)

- **Takeoff [POH/AFH]:** flaps 10° [POH p. 4-14: 10° is for soft or rough fields], keep moving onto the runway, full power, back pressure to get the nosewheel off early. Lift off at the lowest possible speed, then **stay in ground effect** to accelerate to Vx 59 / Vy 73 KIAS [POH p. 4-3] before climbing. With obstacles ahead, leave the 10° flaps extended and climb at 55 KIAS until clear [POH p. 4-15].
- **Landing [AFH]:** flaps 40°, approach 55-65 KIAS [POH p. 4-3 normal flaps-40 range]. Touch down at the slowest possible speed with a little power (**typical**: ~1,200-1,500 RPM, carried through the flare). Hold the nosewheel off, avoid braking, and keep the yoke aft while taxiing.

### Forward slip to landing (IV.M)

- **Config:** power idle, carb heat ON. Flaps per CFI. **POH caution:** "Steep slips should be avoided with flap settings greater than 20°" because the elevator may oscillate under some combinations of airspeed, sideslip angle, and CG [POH p. 4-19; see also crosswind landing, p. 4-20]. So slip with flaps at 20° or less.
- **Airspeed:** normal approach speed, 60-70 KIAS flaps up / 55-65 KIAS flaps 40° [POH p. 4-3].
- **Entry [AFH]:** lower the upwind wing with aileron, apply opposite rudder to keep the longitudinal axis on the runway, and adjust pitch to hold airspeed (the airspeed indicator may read in error in a slip).
- **Recovery:** release the slip smoothly before the flare (neutralize rudder and level the wings), then make a normal landing within 400 ft of the point.

### Go-around / balked landing (IV.N)

- **POH [p. 4-9 checklist, p. 4-20]:** full throttle, carb heat cold, **flaps 20° immediately**, **climb speed 55 KIAS**. Flaps 10° until obstacles are cleared, then retract after reaching a safe altitude and 60 KIAS, and accelerate to the normal flaps-up climb (70-80 KIAS / Vy 73). Above 3,000 ft, lean the mixture for max RPM [p. 4-20].
- **Trim:** strong nose-up pitching tendency at full power with flaps 40°. Hold forward pressure and re-trim [AFH].
- **Path:** sidestep to the side of the runway to keep traffic in sight.
