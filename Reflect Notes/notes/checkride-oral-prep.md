# Checkride Oral Prep

#flying #checkride
Assistant: [[Maverick]]

Starter study plan for the private pilot oral, organized by ACS Areas of Operation (knowledge and risk management elements). See also [[Checkride Maneuvers - ACS Tolerances]] and [[Preston Nelson]].

Examiners use scenario-based questions, often tied to the applicant's own planned cross-country. Be ready to walk through the plan and the reasoning behind it.

## I. Preflight Preparation

### Pilot qualifications
- Certificate privileges and limitations
- Recency/currency requirements
- Medical certificate (class, duration) and BasicMed basics

### Airworthiness requirements
- Required documents: **ARROW** (Airworthiness cert, Registration, Radio station license if international, Operating limitations/POH, Weight & balance)
- Required inspections: **AV1ATE** (Annual, VOR check for IFR, 100-hour if for hire, Altimeter/static, Transponder, ELT)
- Required equipment, 91.205 day VFR: **ATOMATOFLAMES**
- Inoperative equipment / MEL, 91.213

### Weather information
- METAR, TAF, winds aloft, AIRMET, SIGMET, PIREP
- Weather theory: fronts, stability, fog, icing, thunderstorms

### Cross-country planning
- Nav log
- Fuel reserves, 91.151
- VFR cruising altitudes, 91.159

### National Airspace System
- Airspace classes and entry requirements
- VFR weather minimums, 91.155
- Special use airspace

### Performance and limitations
- Weight and balance
- Density altitude
- Takeoff and landing performance charts

### Operation of systems (C172N)
- Engine
- Fuel system
- Electrical system
- Flight instruments and pitot-static system
- Vacuum system

### Human factors
- Hypoxia
- Spatial disorientation
- IMSAFE
- Alcohol and drugs, 91.17

## II. Preflight Procedures
- Preflight inspection, cockpit management, engine start, taxi, before-takeoff check

## III. Airport Operations
- Radio communications
- Airport lighting
- Signs and markings
- LAHSO

## VI. Navigation
- Pilotage and dead reckoning
- VOR and GPS
- Lost procedures

## IX. Emergency Operations
- Engine failure, systems malfunctions, emergency equipment and survival gear

## XI. Night Operations
- Night requirements, lighting, physiology/vision, night illusions

## Risk management
- **PAVE** (Pilot, Aircraft, enVironment, External pressures)
- **3P** (Perceive, Process, Perform)
- **DECIDE** model
