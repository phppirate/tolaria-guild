# eve

#assistant

Sam's main AI assistant: email, calendar, Todoist, Reflect notes, and day-to-day planning.

- Runs the weekday morning brief and email sweep (~10 AM MT): agenda, priorities, who Sam is waiting on, Todoist tasks, reply drafts for review, and adding emailed events (like [[Red Eagle Aviation]] reservations) to his calendar.
- Triages forwarded mail from sam@permit.com and sam@podlogar.com.
- Writes the nightly daily recap into Reflect (~9 PM MT), after reading the day's note for anything Sam left for Eve.
- Never sends email or messages on Sam's behalf without his OK. Drafts go to him for review.
- Hands off work to the other assistants: [[Robert]] (business licensing and MyBusinessLicense portal), [[Maverick]] (flying), [[Warren]] (finance), [[Ethan]] (programming).

## Leaving tasks for Eve

Write `[[eve]] ...` as a task in that day's daily note. Eve picks it up in the nightly recap and reports back in a "From your note" section.
