# C172 Maneuver Setup Card

N73785, a 1977 C172N with a 180 hp Lycoming O-360-A4M (STC SA4428SW). Home field is S27 (2,930 ft). CFI is [[preston-nelson|Preston Nelson]]. Oral prep: [[checkride-oral-prep|Checkride Oral Prep]].

Speeds, RPMs, configurations, flows, and ACS tolerances below come from the **Red Eagle C172N Training Supplement 2026** (emailed Oct 24, 2026), unless marked **(POH)** or ***typical***. All speeds are KIAS. The supplement says it does not replace the POH/AFM, the Airplane Flying Handbook, or the ACS. Items still marked ***typical, confirm with Preston*** are ones the supplement doesn't cover.

## Key numbers (supplement)

- **Vr** 55 · **Vx** 59 (flaps 0) / 55 (flaps 10) · **Vy** 73 (flaps 0) / 68 (flaps 10)
- **Vfe** 85 (10-30°) · **Vno** 128 · **Vne** 160
- **Va** 105 / 95 / 85 at 2,550 / 2,150 / 1,750 lb
- **Vg** 68 / 62 at 2,550 / 2,150 lb in the table, but **65** in every procedure (see Questions)
- Stall speeds as printed: Vs (clean) 41, Vso (landing) 47 (see Questions)
- Max takeoff and landing weight 2,550 lb · combined baggage 120 lb · N73785 empty weight 1,529.9 lb, moment 62,611.53
- CG: forward 40.5 in at 2,550 lb, 35.1 in at <1,950 lb · aft 47.3 in ("at 2500lbs" as printed)
- Fuel 43 gal total, 40 usable · 8 qt oil sump
- **Training cruise: ~95-100 KIAS at ~2,300 RPM.** Use the POH and STC for other cruise settings.

## Takeoffs

On every takeoff, check full power (**min 2,300 RPM**) and engine gauges, then call **"Power set, engine gauges normal, airspeed alive."**

| Takeoff | Setup | Sequence | ACS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Normal** | Flaps 0, line up on centerline | Full throttle, then callout. Rotate 55, pitch for 75 (Vy 73), climb 70-80 | Private Vy +10/-5 · Commercial ±5 |
| **Short field** | Flaps 10°, use all available runway, hold brakes | Full throttle (min 2,300), release brakes, callout. Rotate 55, pitch for 60 (Vx 59). At 400 AGL with positive rate, flaps 0, climb 75 (Vy 73) | Private Vx/Vy +10/-5 · Commercial ±5 |
| **Soft field** | Flaps 10°, yoke full aft, gradual turn onto centerline with minimal braking (keep rolling) | Full throttle, callout. When the nosewheel lifts, ease back pressure but keep a positive AOA. Stay in ground effect (≤20 ft / ½ wingspan), accelerate to Vx, climb 60. At 400 AGL with positive rate, flaps 0, climb 75 | Same as short field. Lift off at the lowest possible speed and stay in ground effect to Vx/Vy |

Turn out beyond the departure end and within 300 ft of pattern altitude, whichever comes later (AIM 4-3-3).

## Pattern and landings

**BCGUMPS (before-landing flow, after the descent and before-landing checklists):** **B**elts on · **C**arb heat ON · **G**as: fuel selector BOTH · **U**ndercarriage down, wheels/tires normal · **M**ixture enriched (best power) · **P**rops/pumps (none on the 172N) · **S**witches: all exterior lights on.

| Leg | Power | Flaps | Speed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Downwind** | ~2,300 RPM, BCGUMPS | 0° | ~90 kt |
| **Abeam the touchdown point** | 1,500 RPM | 10° below 85 | Pitch for 70-80 |
| **Base** | As required | 20° | 60-70 |
| **Final** | Use power to control descent rate, idle crossing the threshold | 30° | 55-60 (1.3 Vso) |

- Stabilized approach, typically **500 fpm**. Keep the aiming point stationary in the windshield with pitch, and adjust power to hold speed. Use "pitch for airspeed, power for altitude" only when the approach isn't yet stabilized.
- **Short-field landing:** 55 on final, power controls descent rate. Aiming point ~500 ft before the touchdown point. Before crossing it, power IDLE and shift to a new aiming point ¾ of the way to touchdown. On touchdown on centerline, simulate max braking with the yoke aft.
- **Soft-field landing:** 55-60 on final, power controls descent rate. Touch down as softly as possible with slight power, then throttle IDLE. Keep the nosewheel off as long as possible, yoke full aft, minimal braking until clear of the runway.
- **ACS:** approach speed Private +10/-5, Commercial +5/-0. Touchdown Private -0/+400 ft on centerline (short field -0/+200) · Commercial -0/+200 ft (short field -0/+100).

**Go-around:** full power, pitch 60 (Vx), **flaps 20°**, carb heat off. Positive climb: flaps 10°, pitch 65. Above 400 AGL with positive rate: flaps 0°, pitch Vy 73. Go around any time the approach is unstable, alignment is off, or there's a hazard on the runway or final.

## CHAPS (before every maneuver)

- **C**learing turns: **two 90° turns to the left, or one 180° turn to the left**.
- **H**eading: outside reference point plus the heading on the HI.
- **A**ltitude: enough for the maneuver. ≥1,500 AGL (≈4,500+ MSL) for stalls and slow flight. 600-1,000 AGL (≈3,600-3,900 MSL) for ground reference.
- **P**ower/pumps/props: set to cruise (no fuel pumps or adjustable prop on this airplane).
- **S**witches/seatbelts: all lights on, seatbelts fastened.

Previous card (AC 61-98) used **P** = place to land. Still pick an emergency field.

## Emergencies

**ALARMS (engine failure)**

- **A**irspeed: best glide **65**.
- **L**anding spot: pick one and set up to land on it.
- **A**ttempt restart, **FLOOR to DOOR**: fuel BOTH, mixture rich, throttle cracked, carb heat ON, mags BOTH/RIGHT/LEFT, master ON. If it won't restart, shut down FLOOR to DOOR: fuel OFF, mixture lean/cutoff, throttle idle, carb heat off, mags OFF. **Leave the master ON for radios and flaps until landing is assured.**
- **R**adios: current frequency, then 121.5. Squawk 7700.
- **M**ayday ×3: tail number, location, engine failure, landing near…, souls on board.
- **S**ecure the airplane for landing. Pull out the emergency checklist.

**Engine fire:** pitch for Vne ("158 KIAS" as printed) and bank away from the flames or smoke. Pick a landing spot. **Do not attempt a restart.** Shut down FLOOR to DOOR, including the master. Once everything is off, pitch for Vg 65 and set up for the landing spot. Radio and Mayday are only possible if you make the call before the master goes off. Secure the airplane and pull out the emergency checklist.

**Electrical:** check the circuit breakers (under the pilot's yoke). An essential breaker may be reset **only once** in flight, and only if there's no smoke or burning smell and the system is needed. Never reset non-essential breakers in flight.

## Commercial maneuvers (later)

- **Accelerated stall:** >3,000 AGL. Carb heat, 1,500 RPM, hold altitude. At 70, roll into 45° and pull smoothly to the buffet. Recover: full throttle, lower the nose, level the wings, Vy 73.
- **Chandelle:** ≥1,500 AGL, ~90-100 KIAS. 30° bank, full throttle, raise pitch to ~15° by the 90° point. Then hold pitch and shallow the bank to wings level at 180°, at minimum controllable airspeed. Rollout ±10°.
- **Lazy eight:** ≥1,500 AGL, ~90-100 KIAS. At 45°: max pitch up, 15° bank. At 90°: level pitch, 30° bank (max). At 135°: max pitch down, ~15° bank. At 180°: entry speed and altitude. ±10 KIAS, ±100 ft, ±10° at each 180° point.
- **Eights on pylons:** pivotal altitude = GS²/11.3, ~90-100 KIAS, enter 45° to downwind between the pylons. Pitch to hold the pylon on the reference line. ≤40° bank.
- **Steep spiral:** start high enough ("3,00' AGL" as printed; see the ACS). Carb heat ON, idle, Vg 65. At least 3 constant-radius turns, ≤60° bank, clear the engine every 360°. Complete ≥1,500 AGL. ±10 KIAS, rollout ±10°.
- **Power-off 180:** BCGUMPS at midfield, normal downwind. Abeam the touchdown point, throttle IDLE, hold altitude until 65, flaps as required. Touch down -0/+200 ft.
- **Commercial tolerances in the private tasks:** steep turns at 50°, slow flight +5/-0, alt ±50, bank ±5°.
