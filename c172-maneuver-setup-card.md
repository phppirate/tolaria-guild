# C172 Maneuver Setup Card

#flying #checkride

N73785, a 1977 C172N with a 180 hp O-360 (STC SA4428SW). Per the STC supplement, POH speeds and procedures are unchanged. Home field is S27 (2,930 ft). CFI is [[preston-nelson|Preston Nelson]]. All speeds are KIAS at 2,300 lb and come from the POH via [[checkride-maneuvers-acs-tolerances|Checkride Maneuvers - ACS Tolerances]]. Anything marked ***typical*** is technique, not a POH number. RPMs are all typical, so **confirm them with Preston**. Oral prep: [[checkride-oral-prep|Checkride Oral Prep]].

## CHAPS (before every maneuver)

- **C**learing turns: two 90° turns or one 180°. Look above, below, and around.
- **H**eading plus an outside reference. Note both.
- **A**ltitude: ≥1,500 AGL (≈4,500+ MSL) for stalls and slow flight. 600-1,000 AGL (≈3,600-3,900 MSL) for ground reference.
- **P**lace to land: pick an emergency field.
- **S**et power and configuration: use one of the flows below.

Source: AC 61-98 via [CFI Commonplace Book](https://publish.obsidian.md/cfi-commonplace-book/Filed/CHAPS). (AC 61-98D's proficiency plan suggests ≥3,000 AGL for airwork. The ACS minimum is 1,500 AGL.)

| **Soft-field takeoff** | Flaps 10° | Full power, carb heat cold | Accelerate in ground effect to Vx 59 / Vy 73. Obstacle: 55 with 10° | Keep rolling onto the runway. Full aft yoke, nosewheel light. Lift off at the lowest speed | Vx/Vy +10/-5 |
| **Soft-field landing** | Flaps 40° | Landing flow; ~1,200-1,500 RPM through touchdown *typical* | 55-65 (flaps 40°) | Touch down at the slowest speed with power on. Nose high, hold the nosewheel off. Minimal or no brakes. Yoke aft while taxiing | Approach +10/-5 |

Other limits: Vfe 85, Vno 128, Vne 160, max demonstrated crosswind 15 kt. Go-around: full power, carb heat cold, **flaps 20°**, 55, then clean up. Stall speeds (forward / aft CG): flaps 0 47/42, 10° 44/38, 40° 41/36. **Avoid slips with flaps >20°.** Normal approach is 60-70 flaps up or 55-65 flaps 40°. Normal climb is 70-80.

## ALARMS (engine failure)

- **A**irspeed: **65 best glide**, trimmed.
- **L**anding area: pick it, into the wind if possible.
- **A**ir restart: carb heat ON, mixture RICH, fuel BOTH, primer IN and locked, mags BOTH (START if the prop has stopped). Follow the POH engine-failure checklist.
- **R**adio: current frequency or 121.5. Squawk 7700.
- **M**ayday: tail number, position, problem, intentions.
- **S**ecure: mixture idle cutoff, fuel OFF, mags OFF, flaps as required (40° for the forced landing, 60 KIAS), master OFF, doors unlatched.

Source: [Pilots of America thread](https://www.pilotsofamerica.com/community/threads/checklist-acronyms-and-sayings.7052/), with item details from POH pp. 3-3/3-4. *Versions vary, so confirm with Preston.* Simulated recovery: carb heat cold, full power, Vy 73.
